Read this first: the one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy launch guides flag as possibly missable: the meditation spot on Lucia’s balcony in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter. There is no mission replay reported for this game, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more right before the final mission. Everything else can be earned after the story. Turning on a cheat disables trophies — finish the hunt before touching cheats.

Sources: Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List (PowerPyx / GTABase flag the missable spot; no mission replay) · CryptoGames — Full List and Hard Ones · Planet GTA 6 (cheats disable trophies, via PowerPyx)
